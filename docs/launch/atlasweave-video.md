## What Court changed

Court acquitted the demonstrated workflow 12–0 on November 22. The previous 0.9.2 toolbar workaround and pending-inline-test language are superseded. Public 0.10.0 was installed, reloaded and tested in the separate QA vault on macOS Obsidian 1.15.1. Reopening the saved board and plain-clicking its inline alias jumped below 80 filler paragraphs to the exact block. See [[PUBLIC-RELEASE-RETEST]] and [[qa-0.10.0/court/VERDICT|Court verdict]].

The script describes observable actions. It does not promise learning gains, reliable behavior for every file/platform, financial outcomes, repeat use, high conversion, or virality. “Explain in your own words” is an invitation, not a measured result. Do not present GitHub availability as Obsidian Community directory approval.

## Recording decisions

Use synthetic content in a recording vault; keep production notes, paths, notifications and personal account details out of the frame. Planning files live in Nexlmac as requested. Use the public 0.10.0 assets, not an unpublished local build. Record the final demo fixture once before filming; the prior successful QA fixture is evidence, not a substitute for rehearsing a newly edited board.

Capture at 3840×2160 / 30 fps if available; deliver 1920×1080. Keep actionable text readable at delivery size. Save a clean capture without captions and a captioned version. Shots 03–04 may trim idle typing; never imply one-click automatic note-to-map conversion. Keep shots 05–06 continuous at real speed. Use the product palette and existing logo. Do not introduce a separate Kairo card, logo, voiceover or end-screen CTA.

The original seasons sample remains an optional future example; it is not the release proof or promised download in this cut. No starter-download claim goes live until a real public sample URL is available and tested.
